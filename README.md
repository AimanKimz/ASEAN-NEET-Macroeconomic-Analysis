# ASEAN-NEET-Macroeconomic-Analysis
Data Challenge 5.0 UKM - Top 20 Finalist Project by Serunding Data.
## 📝 Project Overview
This project investigates the alarming global increase in **NEET** (Not in Employment, Education, or Training) among youths. By bridging macroeconomic indicators with vocational education participation, this analysis aims to diagnose the "skill mismatch" within the ASEAN region and evaluate geographical economic disparities across Malaysian states.

Our final deliverable is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide data-driven recommendations for policymakers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Data Sources
* **Visualization & Analytics:** Power BI (Dual-Axis Charts, Geospatial Mapping, KPI Dashboards)
* **Data Preprocessing:** Power Query / Excel
* **Data Sources:** DOSM, ASEANStats, World Bank

## 💡 Key Insights & Discoveries
1. **The "Skill Mismatch" Phenomenon in ASEAN:** High vocational education participation does *not* automatically reduce NEET rates in developing nations (e.g., Indonesia, Brunei).
2. **The Capital-Intensive Paradox (Terengganu):** Despite having a moderately high GDP from Oil & Gas, Terengganu maintains a high NEET rate (6.8%).
3. **Geographical Disparities:** Melaka successfully maintained a low 3.4% unemployment rate while Sabah suffered a critical 15.4% high.
